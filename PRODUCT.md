# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Alpha is primarily an operator and research workbench for Ajax and collaborating research agents. Its public
surfaces also serve researchers, reviewers, and curious observers who need to inspect the program's evidence,
methods, failures, and current scientific state without relying on a polished summary.

## Product Purpose

Alpha supports the construction and study of a small, genuinely conversational model specialized in language,
meaning, ontology, philosophy, evidence, and conceptual inquiry. The web product makes both the model program
and its synthetic-curriculum ledger inspectable. Success means a visitor can move from a high-level research
claim to the exact table rows, generation artifacts, reviews, rejections, lineage, and exposure records that
support or qualify it.

## Positioning

Alpha is a public, evidence-first research workbench where synthetic data, model behavior, provenance, and
failure remain inspectable rather than being collapsed into a finished benchmark number.

## Brand Personality

Exact, candid, curious, and quietly ambitious. Alpha should speak like a serious research collaborator: direct
about what is known, explicit about what remains unreviewed, and interested in the conceptual structure beneath
the data without becoming theatrical or academic for its own sake.

## Anti-references

Alpha must not resemble an opaque AI marketing dashboard, a decorative philosophy magazine, or a card-heavy
analytics template that hides rows behind summary metrics. It must not imply that generated, structurally valid,
human-reviewed, accepted, released, and training-exposed data are the same state.

## Design Principles

- Make the evidence traversable: summaries lead to tables, rows, artifacts, and lineage.
- Preserve scientific state: rejected, superseded, disputed, and unreviewed material remains visible and clearly
  labelled.
- Prefer dense legibility over decorative spaciousness; this is a working instrument.
- Keep public access read-only and make the absence of mutation controls obvious.
- Reuse Alpha's existing interaction and visual vocabulary so corpus research and model research feel like one
  coherent program.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All core browsing must work by keyboard and on narrow screens, retain visible focus, avoid color-only status
communication, maintain WCAG AA contrast, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Wide scientific tables may
scroll horizontally, but navigation and row inspection must remain usable without precision pointing.
