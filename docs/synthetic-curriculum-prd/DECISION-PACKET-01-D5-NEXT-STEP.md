# Decision Packet 01 — Alpha Corpus after D5 human review

**Status:** unratified template; not an authorization

**Prepared for:** operator decision after PRD-12 review completion

**Current default:** no new calls, generation, release, training, GPU work, or live Donto mutation

## 1. Decision to be made

After all 48 calibration candidates are human-reviewed, decide which smallest next action creates the most
useful evidence for Alpha's synthetic-data program while preserving the project's product aim: a small,
chatty model that communicates intelligently about language, ontology, philosophy, evidence, intent, and
knowledge structure rather than memorizing the world's long tail of facts.

This packet prevents “the samples looked promising” from silently expanding into a large generation campaign.
It also prevents excessive caution from turning the planned synthetic-data factory into a documentation-only
project. The evidence should determine the next bounded step.

## 2. Required evidence attachment

Do not ratify a next step until the decision record links:

- the exact campaign and audit-packet hash;
- all Pass A and Pass B review IDs;
- six family syntheses;
- structural dispositions for all six rejected candidates;
- failure-cluster report;
- conversational-distribution report;
- hidden-repeat and Pass A/B stability report;
- current ledger, repository, and rubric revisions;
- current project-owned storage footprint;
- unresolved disagreement and expertise register.

## 3. What the first calibration can and cannot establish

### It can establish

- whether GPT-5.4 can realize these six family blueprints in natural language;
- which kinds of errors occur in candidate prose versus hidden contracts;
- whether the current schema confuses lenses, transformations, and response policies;
- whether the outputs have distinctive pedagogical and conversational value;
- whether a later critic or batching probe is worth its calls;
- which review dimensions need expert or ordinary-conversation judgment.

### It cannot establish

- that the broader curriculum taxonomy is complete;
- that Alpha will learn from the data;
- that synthetic-only training will produce a chatty model;
- that a critic is calibrated, because no critic has run;
- that GPT-5.4 is optimal for every generation task;
- that GPT-5.5 is or is not worth using on difficult criticism;
- that 48 candidates constitute a release or training corpus;
- that accepted-looking rows are safe to scale without family-level controls.

## 4. Decision options

Options may be combined only when their scopes remain explicit. None is automatic.

### Option A — repair planning only

Choose when human review finds systemic conceptual, conversational, blueprint, or schema problems.

Authorized work if selected:

- revise PRDs, rubric, taxonomy, family blueprints, and generator prompt specifications;
- write migration or implementation proposals;
- preserve the 48-candidate campaign unchanged;
- prepare a replacement-calibration authorization for later operator review.

Not authorized:

- replacement generation;
- model critics;
- training or GPU work.

### Option B — bounded GPT-5.4 batching and prompt probe

Choose when candidate quality is promising but call overhead, batching, or policy diversity needs evidence.

Suggested experimental shape for later ratification:

- use GPT-5.4 only;
- select two human-validated families with different reasoning demands;
- compare single-family calls against attention-visible multi-item or multi-family batching;
- keep target item counts and target-token distributions as matched as practical;
- generate only a small calibration cohort, kept quarantined;
- human-review every output with the same rubric;
- measure conceptual value per call, per output token, and per human-review minute;
- stop after the predeclared call envelope or earlier if errors repeat.

The ratified decision must fill in exact maximum calls, items, families, prompt versions, token/cost boundary,
artifact path, storage check, and stop conditions. “Use the subscription efficiently” is not a bound.

### Option C — bounded critic calibration

Choose only after human judgments exist and there is a clear triage question.

Suggested order:

1. test independently prompted GPT-5.4 criticism against the human reference;
2. inspect dimension-level false accepts, false rejects, overconfidence, and correlated teacher-judge errors;
3. use GPT-5.5 or a different provider only for a small paired subset where GPT-5.4 shows a concrete failure
   and the stronger/diverse critic could change routing decisions;
4. never let a critic promote its own output or replace human authority on contested cases.

The critic output remains review evidence, not a training label unless a later release policy explicitly
selects it.

### Option D — D6 AlphaPact and ordinary-chat evaluation construction

Choose when the reviewed constructs are stable enough to freeze evaluation before further production.

Authorized scope must specify:

- evaluation construct and family allocation;
- human-authored versus generated content boundaries;
- private storage and leakage controls;
- whole-family, lexical, template, teacher, and domain holdouts;
- ordinary-chat response-initiation and conversation-survival suite;
- human adjudication and set-valued answer policy;
- explicit prohibition on using D5 candidates as both training examples and hidden evaluation targets.

D6 is data design and benchmark freezing. It is not model training.

### Option E — later D7 synthetic corpus pilot

This is intentionally not the immediate default. Choose only after D5 is adjudicated and D6 evaluation is
frozen or separately justified.

A D7 authorization must state:

- approved family and projection versions;
- exact maximum calls, items, and subscription/model routes;
- expected distribution of dialogue kinds, lengths, response policies, difficulty, and transformations;
- human-review allocation and maximum unreviewed backlog;
- critic calibration evidence;
- accepted/rejected/disputed retention policy;
- corpus-home storage measurement and 15 GiB soft pause behavior;
- release and training quarantine;
- stop conditions tied to conceptual error clusters and marginal family value.

Production scale is a sequence of evidence-gated cohorts, not a promise to reach an arbitrary row count.

### Option F — stop or redirect

Choose when the curriculum does not produce distinctive value, the human construct is too unstable, or a
different Alpha research direction is more compelling. Preserve all evidence and state the rejected
hypothesis clearly.

## 5. Suggested interpretation matrix

The following is a discussion aid, not an automatic threshold policy:

| Observed pattern | Likely next decision |
|---|---|
| conceptually wrong but fluent across several siblings | Option A; revise or retire blueprint |
| good concepts, recurrent canned voice | Option A, then a tightly scoped Option B |
| good candidates rejected only by field-type confusion | taxonomy/schema repair before more generation |
| human judgments coherent, review workload dominant | Option C critic calibration |
| reviewed constructs stable but production economics unknown | Option B plus Option D |
| strong local quality but weak family diversity | redesign allocation; do not scale rows |
| natural dialogue but little reusable conceptual value | redesign family contracts |
| conceptual value but no conversational presence | preserve research data; redesign the product-facing scenes |
| stable human construct and promising factory economics | Option D before a later bounded Option E |
| reviewers cannot agree for principled reasons | preserve contested set; narrow claims or redirect |

## 6. Model-routing policy for any ratified next step

- GPT-5.6-sol remains the counsel tier for high-leverage family design, diagnosis, rubric revision, and hard
  adjudication preparation.
- GPT-5.4 remains the initial economical surface worker and first critic candidate where the task is bounded.
- GPT-5.5 is not a routine second opinion. Use it only in a paired, small, explicitly bounded probe when human
  evidence identifies a GPT-5.4 weakness important enough to justify escalation.
- Provider or model diversity is useful only when it tests correlated error, not when it merely produces more
  prose.
- Humans remain final authority for contested philosophy, counterexample validity, cultural or linguistic
  authority, and whether a conversation is genuinely worth continuing.

## 7. Synthetic-data factory principle

The long-term target is not a folder of generated chats. It is a reusable scientific substrate from which
different researchers can build training and post-training mixtures by querying:

- concept family and projection;
- linguistic, pragmatic, ontological, philosophical, epistemic, mereological, teleological, temporal, causal,
  social, rhetorical, and discourse lenses;
- dialogue operation and response policy;
- difficulty, length, register, and conversation trajectory;
- positive, negative, borderline, plural, false-bridge, repair, and transfer roles;
- source, license, authority, provenance, and evidence status;
- human/model review and disagreement;
- release membership, rendering, tokenizer exposure, and actual training use.

The project expects to generate much of this material itself through a hierarchy of counsel, economical
workers, critics, deterministic analyzers, and human adjudicators. Cost control comes from spending the
smartest reasoning on structures that propagate across many examples, then using cheaper models for bounded
surface realization and selective repair. It must never come from silently lowering conceptual depth.

## 9. Current recommendation before human review

Complete PRD-12 first. Do not choose a generation or critic option on the basis of structural yield and sample
impressions alone. Once the human reference exists, the smallest likely next package is either repair-only or

critic gap. Training and GPU work remain outside every immediate option in this packet.
