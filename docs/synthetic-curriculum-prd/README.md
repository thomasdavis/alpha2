## The goal in one paragraph

Alpha is to become a small, genuinely chatty model whose scarce internal capacity is spent on language,
meaning, pragmatics, ontology, philosophy, evidence, conceptual negotiation, and the structure of knowledge
rather than encyclopedic fact recall. Roughly half of the program is the deliberate construction of a large,
beautifully categorized synthetic conversational curriculum. That curriculum will be generated and reviewed
by a cost-aware hierarchy of strong and economical models, stored in an immutable SQLite scientific ledger,
and released so other researchers can construct their own training and post-training mixtures. The first
controlled Alpha experiment will train only on material generated by this system. Donto supplies the
abundance-native intellectual method: inspect a situation through many lenses, mint distinctions freely,
retain incompatible analyses, anchor claims to evidence, and defer alignment or collapse until the question
requires it. Conversation remains the product; all formal machinery stays behind the scenes.

## Program invariants

- **Conversation is the product.** Alpha must feel responsive, present, adaptive, and worth continuing to
  talk with. Ontological sophistication cannot excuse empty, stiff, or lecture-like responses.
- **Synthetic-data construction is a principal workstream.** It is not disposable preprocessing and not a
  small appendix to training.
- **The ledger keeps the full scientific record.** Accepted, rejected, disputed, regenerated, transformed,
  rendered, and exposed artifacts remain reconstructable.
- **Model-visible text is natural language.** Database schemas, JSON, delimiters, labels, and hidden formal
  states exist for research control; they are not Alpha's conversational voice.
- **Donto-style abundance remains open.** Taxonomies guide coverage but do not limit what distinctions a
  generator may discover. New lenses and predicates can be proposed, reviewed, versioned, and reused.
- **Plurality is legal state.** Genuine ambiguity, rival theories, conflicting testimony, and perspective-
  relative classification are not collapsed solely to manufacture a single answer.
- **Facts are externalizable; conceptual competence is not.** Alpha should reason well with supplied
  evidence and know when retrieval is needed. It is not required to memorize the world's long tail of
  entity-specific facts.
- **Single-GPU feasibility is a hard engineering constraint.** The project deliberately avoids a fixed
  parameter-count identity. Architecture and scale are selected by measured conversational gain within one
  available GPU.
- **The first causal training study is synthetic-only.** Human material may calibrate reviewers and frozen
  evaluation, but it does not enter the primary training mixture unless a later experiment explicitly says so.
- **No generation is approved merely by this document.** The operator separately authorized and completed the
  bounded D2–D5 calibration recorded in Execution 01. Any expansion still requires a new bounded decision.
- **The scientific ledger is publicly inspectable, not publicly mutable.** The operator separately authorized
  the read-only explorer recorded in PRD-11 and Execution 02. Public visibility does not promote a candidate,
  create a release, or authorize training.

## What “comprehensive” means here

The suite specifies the research objects, taxonomies, provenance, orchestration, quality gates, exports,
experiments, and failure criteria far enough that another capable research agent can criticize or implement
one bounded part without silently inventing the program. It does not pretend every scientific choice is
settled. Open questions are first-class records, not gaps to hide.
